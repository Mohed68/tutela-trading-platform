# First Production Transaction Runbook

## Before admitting the pilot

1. Confirm deployed commit, migration `0024`, service health, backup, Platform Owner and MFA/recent-step-up.
2. Obtain counsel approval for the specific parties, governing law, CISG choice, ICC arbitration seat/language/count, sanctions/export controls, banking language, force-majeure/hardship wording and Urea profile.
3. Verify both legal Organizations through Policy V2 independent review; confirm current Trust, active owner Membership and Participation eligibility.
4. Grant signing authority only to the intended named signers under recent step-up. Do not grant Admin or support staff party authority.
5. Agree out-of-band incident contacts. Confirm no raw protected documents will be uploaded where only metadata/reference support exists.

## Transaction procedure

Seller creates the Urea 46% SELL Offer, supplies supported evidence metadata, submits and waits for canonical verification/publication. Buyer selects the published Offer and creates the Order. Seller validates quantity and accepts. A participant opens the Contract, enters only explicitly agreed terms and resolves every server readiness item. Both parties inspect the exact preview and hash; Seller then Buyer perform fresh MFA step-up, consent and sign the exact version. Download the executed artifact and independently retain its reported SHA-256.

An authorized party starts execution and records documentary references. Buyer confirms delivery only after its operational evidence review. Seller confirms settlement only after its own bank/treasury confirmation; TUTELA moves no funds. Close only when delivery and settlement are confirmed and no dispute is open. If facts are contested, open a dispute and stop closeout; do not edit prior events or the executed artifact.

## Monitoring and incident stop conditions

Operations inspects the safe Admin projection for state, versions, readiness, signatures, hashes, evidence metadata and disputes; it never fabricates party actions. Stop the pilot and preserve all records for any identity/authority doubt, version/hash mismatch, suspected duplicate allocation, sanctions concern, material evidence dispute, service/database integrity alert or legal instruction. Do not repair by direct SQL. Back up first and use the incident/recovery path.

## Completion evidence

Record Contract ID/version/snapshot ID, preview and executed hashes, signer Organizations/users/times, execution/delivery/settlement event IDs, evidence references, closeout event, deployed commit and operator approvals. Never record TOTP codes, secrets, banking credentials or private keys.
