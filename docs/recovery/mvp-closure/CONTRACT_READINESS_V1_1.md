# Contract Readiness V1.1

`READY` and `NOT_READY` are canonical deterministic results. A percentage is presentation only and is calculated from the requirements applicable to the selected commodity/legal/payment profile.

The policy rejects empty values and placeholders including TBD, TBC, pending, unknown, to be agreed, mutually agreed and to be confirmed. `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` is permitted during preparation but blocks readiness, approval and signing until the parties record an explicit CISG choice.

The policy validates positive quantity and unit price, tolerance from 0 to 100, commodity-profile units/specifications, shipment chronology, currency consistency, method-specific payment conditions, the complete applicable document set, inspection and claim rules, default, liability, force majeure, hardship, trade compliance and dispute configuration.

Customs/HS classification is applicable-policy driven and is not a universal blocker. Content readiness does not grant party approval or signature readiness; those remain distinct governed stages.
