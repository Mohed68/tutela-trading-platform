# TUTELA International Commodity Sale Contract — v1

## Product contract

The template is an original TUTELA international sale contract rendered only from an immutable server snapshot. It is ICC-aligned in configuration but is not an “ICC Contract” and contains no copied ICC model-contract text.

Part A records contract/reference/version/snapshot provenance; legal parties and identifiers; representatives and addresses; commodity/specification/origin/producer; quantity/tolerance; price/currency/basis/value; Incoterms® 2020 rule and named place; shipment/packaging/partial shipment; inspection; payment; documents; separate risk and title transfer; governing law; explicit CISG treatment; arbitration configuration; force-majeure/hardship treatment; fees and special conditions.

Part B contains 34 balanced original clauses covering formation, party obligations, conformity, delivery, inspection/claims, documents/payment, risk/title, compliance, force majeure/hardship, default/remedies, confidentiality, assignment, notices, amendments, entire agreement, law/CISG/disputes, electronic contracting, counterparts, precedence and survival.

Annexes are generated only from accepted terms:

- Annex A — Product Specification
- Annex B — Delivery / Shipment Schedule
- Annex C — Required Documents
- Annex D — Inspection Protocol

The preview states that it is not executed and exposes the exact preview SHA-256 through the API. Each signature binds the snapshot ID, contract version and preview hash. After the second valid signature, the server creates one immutable executed PDF with signer identity, Organization, timestamp and signature fingerprint; its SHA-256 is persisted and returned. Rendering is deterministic for the same snapshot/signature inputs.

## Legal boundary

Incoterms allocate specified delivery, risk, cost and obligations; title, payment, governing law and dispute resolution remain explicit separate terms. CISG selection is never inferred from countries. The supported values are applies where legally applicable, expressly included, expressly excluded, or legal review required. The product records electronic consent and audit evidence but makes no qualified-signature claim. Counsel must validate the chosen law, arbitration configuration, CISG choice, banking terms and commodity/jurisdiction requirements for a real pilot transaction.
