# Contract Engine V1.1 Authority

Status: active prospectively for newly prepared contract snapshots.

Seller and Buyer are the only sale parties. TUTELA is a digital contracting, execution-workflow and integrity-record platform. It is not seller, buyer, guarantor, bank, escrow provider, customs authority or legal adviser.

## Formation and integrity

Part A, General Conditions 1-34 and Annexes A-D form one versioned snapshot. Party approval, signing authority and recent MFA step-up are separate gates. The actual governed signature event supplies executed signatory identity; editable display text cannot replace it. Both signatures must bind the same snapshot, version and preview hash.

Executed snapshots and artifacts remain immutable. A post-execution change is a separate linked instrument referring to the parent contract and snapshot. V1.1 does not claim to provide a full amendment lifecycle.

Incoterms 2020 allocates delivery, risk, costs and specified obligations only. Title, payment, governing law and dispute resolution remain separately stated. Customs classifications are references/candidates unless independently established by the responsible declarant or authority.

## Precedence

Part A prevails, followed by General Conditions and then Annexes, unless an explicit Specific Condition states otherwise. Integrity metadata is presented separately in the TUTELA Execution & Integrity Record.
